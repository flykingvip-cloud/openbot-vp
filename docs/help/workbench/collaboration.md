# Collaboration spaces

Walked on 2026-09-18 while logged in. `数字人战队` and its `成员管理` panel were opened.

## What this is

The sidebar group of existing spaces, plus a member panel. The create wizard was not re-opened on this pass.

## How to enter

Sidebar `协作空间`. This group has a plus button. Names on this account included `权变方案组`, `数字人战队`, `市场研究组`, and `产品测试组`. Do not say the list is empty.

Click a space name. `数字人战队` sets the header to `TO: 数字人战队`. People and robots appear on the stage.

## Member panel

Button: `成员管理`. Title: `成员管理——` plus the space name. This space showed `2 位成员 / 4 个机器人`.

| Control               | What it does                                                                                                              | Risk   |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| `添加成员`            | Opens add-person. Not submitted.                                                                                          | Medium |
| `添加机器人`          | Adds an **existing** robot to this space. Not submitted. This is not the sidebar `AI 助手` plus that creates a new robot. | Medium |
| `移交管理权`          | On a non-owner human. Not pressed.                                                                                        | High   |
| `移除`                | On a human or a robot. Not pressed.                                                                                       | High   |
| `AI 协助调整（可选）` | Optional assist block. Not used.                                                                                          | Low    |

A pending invite can read `已发送邀请` and `待主人确认`.

## Not on screen

No tab bar named `概览`, `聊天`, `治理`, or `胶囊`. Do not describe those pages.

The plus button's wizard was not re-opened. If the user wants a new space, confirm the title is `新建协作空间` before naming fields.
