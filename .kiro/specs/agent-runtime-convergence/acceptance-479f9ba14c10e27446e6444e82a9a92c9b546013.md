## Checklist §12

Answered separately. One pass does not cover the others.

1. **Can every task-control behavior name one authority and a real execution site?** Not for every behavior. Pause and cancel on the session board are enforced by `checkTaskActionBoundary` before tool execute. Collaboration claim edges are in policy and the claim lock. Direct answers stop in `routeInteraction` and never reach admission. A full map of every control phrase in every host was not re-proven.

2. **Can every state write be traced to a controlled transaction, with partial failure defined?** Session-board completion and claim-board locks have tests for partial failure. Group delivery and member execution do not. This is not true for every write.

3. **Does ordinary conversation stay out of task execution, and are revision and control free of a second classifier?** Reply and clarify do not authorize or commit in `routeInteraction`. `classifyTurnRelation` is not on that path. An end-to-end `get-reply-run` respond test that asserts the runner was not called was not in this command.

4. **Do scenes share a controlled lifecycle without one forced parameter shape?** Command, cron, followup, and admitted chat keep separate candidate entries. That split is the approved design, not evidence that the structure is missing. This follow-up re-ran existing consumer tests for those entries; it still does not prove every responsibility boundary is consistent.

5. **Are rules queryable and free of scripted config?** The policy catalog is read-only metadata. No config-script engine was added. Coverage of every rule was not re-audited.

6. **Were necessary historical designs kept, and duplicates archived instead of force-moved?** Retirement disposition keeps the listed facades. Nothing was deleted in Batch E. That is not the same as archive completion.

7. **Do Gateway and old facades still hide a second task authority?** Not proven absent. Facades remain on purpose. `session-task-board.ts` still re-exports. No second live classifier was promoted.

8. **Are retained CLI, embedded, and collaboration backends identified rather than counted as a second main line?** Yes as an inventory statement: CLI is `runCliAgent`, embedded is `runEmbeddedPiAgent`, collaboration board writes stay on the claim transaction. They were not re-run here.

9. **Are the three boards separate, and are acceptance and delivery separate?** Session board, claim contract, and private todolist stay separate in source. Acceptance failure does not by itself confirm delivery in the reply-completion test. Group delivery separation was not re-proven end to end.

10. **Is the current SHA evidence complete, with real-run gaps stated apart from source conclusions?** Source simulations above passed on `479f9ba14c10e27446e6444e82a9a92c9b546013`. Real model, real channel, and real PPT are gaps. Source structure is not production go-live.
