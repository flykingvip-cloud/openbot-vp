## Consumption contract

`ControlPlaneApplicationConsumption` in `packages/evolution-core/src/application-consumption.ts`.

`wouldConsume=true` only when Proposal, Experiment, and Application exist and bind; the proposal has passed the application gate (`APPROVED_FOR_APPLICATION` / `CANARY` / `ACTIVE` / `VERIFIED`); surface, adapter, target, and tenant match; the Candidate Store row matches the proposal candidate, the experiment freeze, and the intent payload (hash recomputed from the payload); the grant is approved, unexpired, and its `allowedActions` include the requested action; Proposal and Experiment (including the freeze) are unexpired at `now`; kill switch is off; mode is `auto`; `evaluateSurfaceAutonomy` returns ok for the registered surface; `rollbackRef` is available; and the application is unconsumed or the same idempotency key is replayed.

`wouldConsume=true` still sets `executedConsumer: false`. Nothing is Applied.

There is no `surfaceAutonomyApproved` boolean. Autonomy is `evaluateSurfaceAutonomy` on the registered surface. A caller-supplied policy with `mode: "auto"` does not override `autoApply=false` or `riskLevel: "critical"`. Registered surfaces therefore stay denied with `autonomy_not_approved`. Same-key replay cannot skip that decision.

## Kill switch and rollback evidence

`drillKillSwitchDenial` on an otherwise consumable binding returns `kill_switch` and `wouldConsume=false`.

`drillMissingRollbackDenial` returns `rollback_missing` and `wouldConsume=false`.

Neither drill calls Adapter.rollback or the live mutators.

## Readiness

`evaluateCutoverReadiness` counts every `ConsumptionDenyReason`. `cutoverReady` and `e52SwitchAllowed` are true only when that total is zero, every observation passed grant-action, expiry, Candidate Store, and surface-policy checks, the window has a real `wouldConsume` match, and the kill-switch, rollback, and idempotent-replay drills passed. Candidate, binding, and surface denials block the switch even when target, tenant, and action counters are zero. A row that claims `wouldConsume` without those checks stays closed.

An empty window, a single orphan, any deny reason, or missing drill receipts stay **false**. The live reader omits drill receipts, so production `e52SwitchAllowed` stays **false**.
